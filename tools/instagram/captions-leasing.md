# Evolution24 Properties on Instagram: Now renting

Nine posts for a leasing push, each one complete on its own. Five are listings, at the corners and
in the center: a building's photo with a tan "Now available" stamp that says how many of its homes
are free, and a card at the foot with the city, the building, the kinds of home and the rent they
start at. Between them, four statements make a cross of big words on ink and tan: *Now renting.*
*Now available.* *Now touring.* *Apply online.*

## Before you post: check the numbers

The counts, kinds of home and rents on the posts come from the website's "Available now" list,
and the run prints the date of that list. If anything has leased or changed since, update the
site's list first, then make the posts again:

```bash
python3 tools/instagram/leasing.py
```

A listing whose building has nothing left stops the run, so a full building never goes out as
"Now available". The captions below leave the numbers to the pictures, so they stay true.

## How to post the grid

Instagram puts the newest post at the top left, so you build the grid from the bottom right up.

1. **Post them in file order, 01 first and 09 last,** so the listings land at the corners and in
   the center. Post each one as a single photo, not a carousel.
2. **Keep the whole picture.** The posts are 3:4 (1080 × 1440), the shape of the profile grid.
   Before you tap Next, check that the preview shows the whole post. If it shows a square, tap
   the crop button at the bottom left of the preview.
3. **Add the details for each post:** the caption, the location, and the alt text. Alt text is
   under Advanced settings → Accessibility → Write alt text, and each one below fits Instagram's
   100-character limit.
4. **Unpin anything pinned.** Pinned posts sit at the top of the grid and push the nine out of
   their places. If a post lands in the wrong spot, tap and hold it, choose Reorder grid, and drag
   it into place.
5. **When a building fills up, archive its post** (⋯ → Archive). It leaves the grid but keeps its
   likes and comments, and you can bring it back the next time that building has a home free.
6. **Post from the Instagram app.** Some scheduling tools crop 3:4 images to 4:5.

`grid-gaps.jpg` shows the nine as the profile will, and `profile-preview.png` shows them on a
phone in light and dark mode. Instagram now counts only five hashtags per post, so each caption
has five or fewer.
